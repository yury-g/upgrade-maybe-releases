# Upgrade, Maybe 2.2.0 Build 18

Build 18 makes the menu-bar opinion reflect recovery instead of allowing one old difficult minute to linger as **Hmm, Maybe**.

## Fresh recovery

- The immediate opinion now uses the latest five valid observed minutes.
- After five fresh comfortable minutes, the menu bar returns to **Champ**.
- Brief thermal or workload pressure remains visible while it is recent, then clears when the Mac demonstrates sustained recovery.

## Long-term evidence remains intact

- Fifteen or more constrained minutes can still support deeper **Try This First** guidance.
- Repeated-session evidence, Tune comparisons, health checks, benchmarks, and the two-workday upgrade safeguards remain unchanged.
- Build 17 history is preserved during the Build 18 upgrade.

## Install

1. Download and open `Upgrade-Maybe-2.2.0-build-18-Public-Alpha.dmg`.
2. Double-click **Upgrade, Maybe.pkg**.
3. Follow the standard macOS Installer steps.
4. Use your Mac normally. The app continues observing locally from the menu bar.

Apple silicon and macOS 13.5 or newer are required. The installer and DMG are Developer ID signed, Apple-notarized, stapled, and accepted by Gatekeeper.
