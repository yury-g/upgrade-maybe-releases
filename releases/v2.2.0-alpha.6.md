Upgrade, Maybe 2.2.0 Build 15 makes the menu-bar verdict faster to read while preserving the same local evidence and assessment rules.

### Clearer menu-bar verdict

- The approved Rally Flags trophy replaces the generic computer symbol in the menu bar.
- A comfortable result now shows **Champ** in the Rally Flags green.
- Learning or paused states show **Learning**.
- Brief Signal, Try This First, and Upgrade Could Help states show **Hmm, Maybe**.
- The complete six-state assessment, evidence, action, and history remain available inside the app and Evidence Dashboard.

### Reliability

- The Rally Flags mark is pre-rendered at a bounded Retina size to avoid the first implementation's excessive graphics allocation.
- Browser release-soak diagnostics count only new fetch failures during the measured run.
- The full Build 15 verifier, native model/engine checks, signed-package checks, and measured five-minute soak passed before publication.

### Install

1. Download and open the DMG.
2. Double-click **Upgrade, Maybe.pkg** with the Rally Flags icon.
3. Follow the standard macOS Installer steps.
4. Use your Mac normally. Upgrade, Maybe continues observing locally from the menu bar.

### Requirements

- Apple-silicon Mac
- macOS 13.5 or newer
