Upgrade, Maybe is a local Mac upgrade reality check. This is the first public alpha of the native menu-bar app and its local Evidence Dashboard.

### What it does

- Gives a preliminary opinion after five valid observed minutes.
- Develops a deeper session opinion after five valid observed hours.
- Keeps an upgrade verdict locked until repeated evidence qualifies across two adequately observed workdays.
- Shows Workload Comfort and Strain using the same local AssessmentV1 decision authority in the native app and Evidence Dashboard.
- Keeps metrics and history on your Mac. No account, cloud upload, or credit card is required.

### Install

1. Download and open the DMG.
2. Open the **Upgrade, Maybe 2.2.0 Public Alpha** folder.
3. Double-click **Install Upgrade Maybe.command**.
4. Use your Mac normally. Upgrade, Maybe will open from the menu bar and continue observing locally.

### Requirements

- Apple-silicon Mac
- macOS 13.5 or newer

The DMG, app, bundled runtime, and native helpers are Developer ID signed. The DMG is notarized and stapled by Apple. This remains alpha software: treat its conclusions as decision support, not a hardware guarantee. Optional signals that macOS cannot expose reliably remain labeled unavailable rather than estimated.
