Upgrade, Maybe 2.2.0 Build 10 replaces the Build 9 first-click installer with a standard Apple Installer package.

### Installation fix

- The first-click entry point is now **Install Upgrade, Maybe.pkg**.
- The installer package is signed with Developer ID Installer, notarized by Apple, and stapled.
- The enclosing DMG remains Developer ID signed, notarized, and stapled.
- A quarantined copy of the finished DMG was mounted and the actual installer package was accepted by Gatekeeper before publication.

### What the app does

- Gives a preliminary opinion after five valid observed minutes.
- Develops a deeper session opinion after five valid observed hours.
- Keeps an upgrade verdict locked until repeated evidence qualifies across two adequately observed workdays.
- Uses one local AssessmentV1 authority for the native menu-bar app and Evidence Dashboard.
- Keeps metrics and history on the Mac. No account, cloud upload, or credit card is required.

### Install

1. Download and open the DMG.
2. Double-click **Install Upgrade, Maybe.pkg**.
3. Follow the standard macOS Installer steps.
4. Use your Mac normally. Upgrade, Maybe continues observing locally from the menu bar.

### Requirements

- Apple-silicon Mac
- macOS 13.5 or newer
