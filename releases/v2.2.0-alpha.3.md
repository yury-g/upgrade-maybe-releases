Upgrade, Maybe 2.2.0 Build 11 makes the Rally Flags Statue the app and installer identity, while preserving the signed, notarized Apple Installer flow from Build 10.

### Rally Flags identity

- The macOS app now uses the Rally Flags Statue as its application icon.
- **Install Upgrade, Maybe.pkg** uses the same Rally Flags icon in Finder.
- The disk image carries the same branded volume identity.
- The installer package and final DMG are Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and stapled.

### What the app does

- Gives a preliminary opinion after five valid observed minutes.
- Develops a deeper session opinion after five valid observed hours.
- Keeps an upgrade verdict locked until repeated evidence qualifies across two adequately observed workdays.
- Uses one local AssessmentV1 authority for the native menu-bar app and Evidence Dashboard.
- Keeps metrics and history on the Mac. No account, cloud upload, or credit card is required.

### Install

1. Download and open the DMG.
2. Double-click **Install Upgrade, Maybe.pkg** with the Rally Flags icon.
3. Follow the standard macOS Installer steps.
4. Use your Mac normally. Upgrade, Maybe continues observing locally from the menu bar.

### Requirements

- Apple-silicon Mac
- macOS 13.5 or newer
